03 / How I work

From problem to value.

Always in the same order. First I understand what needs deciding and how it happens in day-to-day operations; technology comes afterwards. That way projects can be explained, measured and maintained.

  1. 01

    Problem

    What decision needs to be made, and who makes it.

  2. 02

    Process

    How it happens today in operations, inside and outside SAP.

  3. 03

    Data

    Where each piece of data originates and what its reliable source is.

  4. 04

    Access

    Who should see what, and why. Security by design.

  5. 05

    Technology

    The right architecture for the problem, no more and no less.

  6. 06

    Measurement

    KPIs defined with the business that can actually be checked.

  7. 07

    Value

    Better, faster decisions with a single version of the data.

In SAP environments

From data in SAP to the decision.

My day-to-day happens in SAP environments. Data originates in operations; I extract it from its reliable source, model it so every department speaks the same language, and turn it into information people can use to decide.

  1. 01

    Understand the process

    Before extracting anything, I understand how operations are recorded in SAP: modules, documents and the business logic behind each table.

  2. 02

    Extract with judgement

    CDS Views and BAPIs to get the data from its reliable source, without duplicating logic or relying on manual exports.

  3. 03

    Model in Datasphere

    Model layers in SAP Datasphere and SQL Server, with shared definitions and data quality checks.

  4. 04

    Bring it to the decision

    A platform such as Microsoft Fabric and dashboards in Power BI, Qlik or SSRS, with well-defined access.

Alongside my work in data, I design and build custom applied-AI, app and web projects. I use the same method I apply to the data of a €300M company: understand the problem, get the architecture right and deliver something that works and can be measured.

Assistants that answer using your company's documents and data (RAG) and connect to your tools through MCP servers. For example, to find similar projects in a historical archive or to prepare documentation from drawings, reports or project specifications.
